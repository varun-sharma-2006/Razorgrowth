RazorGrowth Simulator

Privacy

RazorGrowth is a simulator built for the Razorpay AI Buildathon 2026. It uses Google sign-in only to know who you are, so it can save your simulation runs and show your name on the leaderboard.

What we receive from Google

  • Your name, email address and profile photo URL.
  • Google's identifier for your account, so we recognise you next time.

We never see your Google password, and nothing else in your Google account is accessed.

What we store

  • Your profile details above and the time you last signed in.
  • Your current simulation run. Starting a new run replaces the previous one; runs untouched for 30 days are deleted.
  • Leaderboard entries you choose to submit, under the nickname you enter.
  • A sign-in session (an httpOnly cookie) that lasts 30 days or until you log out.

What we don't do

  • No payments: the customers and payments in the simulator are fictional, and links go through a demo gateway.
  • No advertising, tracking pixels or selling of data.

Removing your data

Log out to end your session on that device. To have your account, runs and leaderboard entries deleted, contact the team through the project's GitHub repository.
